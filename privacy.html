Privacy

Your fitness data should stay understandable.

This demo explains the product privacy model AIGYM is designed around.

What we use

Profile details, workout activity, nutrition entries, progress measurements and preferences used to power your dashboard and recommendations.

What you control

Notifications, connected apps, local demo data, plan selection and profile preferences can be changed from Settings.

Connected data

Health-data integrations should always be permission-based and revocable from Connected Apps.

Data export

The Web App includes a Data & Backup section that can export local demo state as JSON.
